Three collections, and honestly not much connects them beyond my having made
them and still liking how they look.

Crystals grown on a slide and photographed through crossed filters, where all
the colour is a trick of the light. Curves that come out of equations. And a set
of sheets that fold like cloth, built from a few thousand thin lines apiece.
