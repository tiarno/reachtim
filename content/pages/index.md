I've been writing software for about twenty-five years, most of it in Python and
a surprising amount of it around the guts of documents — PDF, LaTeX, XML. It's a
stranger specialty than it sounds, and I've never really wanted to stop.

I wrote the second edition of *Black Hat Python*, and I turn up at security
conferences now and then. The rest of the time I'm making pictures: crystals
under a microscope, curves drawn by arithmetic, and some sheets of folded colour.

Writing on one side, pictures on the other. Go wherever you like.
