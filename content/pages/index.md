I write about Python, the insides of PDF and LaTeX, and monitoring systems that
have to stay up. I also draw: sheets built from several hundred ruled strokes,
where the line spacing does all the work of describing form.

Both halves of this site are the same interest in structure, worked in different
materials. Take either one.
